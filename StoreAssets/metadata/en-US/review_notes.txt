BlurFollow is a macOS utility that places visual masks on screen and updates a mask as its selected window moves or resizes. It has no login or proprietary account. Five saved masks are free; creating a sixth uses a one-time Non-Consumable.

In-App Purchase
• Product ID: com.hinoshiba.blurfollow.unlimited_masks
• Product: BlurFollow Unlimited Masks (one-time, not a subscription)
• Settings → Support BlurFollow always exposes the localized price, purchase, and Restore Purchases.
• Existing masks are never deleted or disabled based on purchase state; only new creation is gated.

Review flow
1. Launch the app and complete onboarding.
2. From Home, choose Display Pin and drag on the selection overlay. Display Pin requires no Screen Recording permission.
3. Choose Window Pin, then select a non-sensitive test window in Apple's system picker. Move and resize the source and verify that the mask updates from its proportional coordinates.
4. In Masks, adjust Strength for Frost or Mosaic. Choose Move…, drag the mask itself, and release to save; start Move… again and press Esc to cancel. From the menu-bar list, switch one mask off and on while leaving Show Masks enabled.
5. Create five Display Pins in total, then choose Display Pin a sixth time. Before range selection, verify Add More Masks shows StoreKit's localized price, Not Now, and Restore Purchases. Complete the purchase with the review account; the original Display Pin selection resumes and permits the sixth mask.
6. Open Share Guide and create Share Preview. Select the same test window in the system picker.
7. Inspect the matching Window Pin in BlurFollow Share Preview and select “I checked every mask.” Changing the source or a mask may briefly clear or cover the preview while the current composite is prepared, and resets that acknowledgement.
8. Stop or close Share Preview and verify that its frame is cleared and capture stops.

Permissions
• macOS 15.2 or later: Apple's system picker authorizes each selected window.
• macOS 14 through 15.1: broader Screen Recording permission is required to identify the selected window. After granting it, quit and reopen BlurFollow. If permission is denied, BlurFollow stops instead of guessing a window.
• The app does not request Accessibility, Full Disk Access, camera, or microphone access.

Data handling
Share Preview processes the selected window frames on device. It does not capture audio, write a screen recording, send frames to the developer, or include analytics or advertising SDKs. Mask settings and limited selected-window identity metadata are stored locally.

Display caveat
“Preview active” reports that a current composited frame is displayed; it does not certify the content. Evaluation requires visual inspection in both BlurFollow Share Preview and the receiving preview of the sharing application. Single-window and browser-tab sharing normally exclude another app's overlay, so the reviewer should share BlurFollow Share Preview rather than the original source in that workflow.

Any non-sensitive test window can be used. No special account or server configuration is required.
