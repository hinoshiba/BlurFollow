Two coordinate modes

Pin it. Follow it. Inspect it.

Match the sharing method with two placement modes and a separate preview you can check.

D

Display Pin

For notification areas and fixed layouts. Place a mask at a proportional position on one display.

W

Window Pin

For moving windows. Update a mask's position and size from proportional window coordinates.

P

Share Preview

Composite matching Window Pins on your Mac and inspect the result in a separate regular window.

G

Share Guide

See the different workflows for full-display, single-window, and browser-tab sharing.

3

Three visual styles

Choose Frost, Mosaic, or Redact for an area you have visually identified.

L

Last-position cover

Optionally cover the latest position known to the app after tracking stops. It may not be current.

Inside the app

A clear path to the receiving preview.

Single-window workflow

Share Share Preview—not the original window.

A desktop overlay from another app is normally excluded from single-window capture in apps such as Chrome and from browser-tab sharing.

Sharing a full display

Inspect BlurFollow's desktop masks, then inspect the meeting application's own preview.

Single window or browser tab

Composite the source and its Window Pins in Share Preview, then select BlurFollow Share Preview in the meeting app.

Check before presenting

Three steps to the share button.

STEP 01

Place the area

Choose Display Pin or Window Pin and drag over an area you have inspected.

STEP 02

Inspect the composite

For a single window, open Share Preview and visually inspect the source, layout, and every mask.

STEP 03

Check the receiving preview

Select the intended target in the meeting app and inspect that app's own receiving preview again.

BlurFollow is a visual aid.

It is not a security control and does not automatically detect confidential information. It does not guarantee mask placement, blur unreadability, or inclusion in the shared output. After scrolling, zooming, opening child windows, or changing capture methods, always inspect both BlurFollow and the meeting application's preview.

Check permissions and compatibility.

Display Pin works without Screen Recording permission. Window Pin and Share Preview use different authorization paths depending on the macOS version.
