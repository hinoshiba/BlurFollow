# BlurFollow community code of conduct

## Our standard

The BlurFollow community is committed to a focused, welcoming, and safe
environment. Treat people with respect, assume good faith while checking facts,
and make room for different backgrounds, identities, experience levels,
languages, accessibility needs, and viewpoints.

Constructive participation includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- giving specific, actionable review and accepting correction gracefully;
- respecting consent, privacy, attribution, and licensing;
- using synthetic examples instead of exposing screen content or personal data;
- clearly separating project facts from personal opinion; and
- helping de-escalate disagreement and welcoming new contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking,
sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption,
bad-faith impersonation, undisclosed conflicts used to manipulate decisions,
publishing another person's private information, or encouraging others to do
any of these things.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, reviews, issues, discussions,
events, chat, social spaces operated by the project, and when someone is
officially representing BlurFollow. It also applies to private conduct when that
conduct creates a credible safety risk for community participation.

## Reporting and enforcement

For immediate danger, contact appropriate local services. For community
misconduct, privately contact the repository maintainers through the canonical
repository's moderation/reporting tools. Do not use a public issue if doing so
would expose a target or sensitive evidence. Security vulnerabilities belong in
the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will limit disclosure to people needed to assess and act, while
recognizing that absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. A maintainer
who is involved in a report must recuse themselves. Knowingly false or
retaliatory reports are themselves violations; an unproven good-faith report
is not.

Proportionate actions may include a private correction, content edit or
removal, warning, temporary participation limits, removal from a role, or a
permanent ban. Decisions consider impact, pattern, intent, repair, and community
safety. When feasible, the decision will identify the violated rule and an
appeal path to an uninvolved maintainer. Retaliation against reporters,
witnesses, or responders is prohibited.

Project maintainers are responsible for applying this policy consistently, not
for resolving unrelated personal disputes. This document creates community
expectations and is not a substitute for legal advice or emergency support.
